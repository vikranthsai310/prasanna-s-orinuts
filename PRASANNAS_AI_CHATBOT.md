## 🎯 How to Use

### Step 1: Open the Chatbot
1. Go to **Admin → Manage Products**
2. Click **"Add New Product"**
3. Click the **"✨ AI Assistant"** button (bottom-right)
4. Chatbot opens - **form stays open!**

### Step 2: Ask Questions
Type naturally like chatting:

**Examples:**
```
"What's the nutritional info for almonds?"
"Tell me about cashews"
"Write a description for dates"
"How many calories in walnuts?"
"What are the health benefits of pistachios?"
```

### Step 3: Copy & Paste
1. AI responds with information
2. Click **"Copy"** button on response
3. Paste into product form fields
4. Continue filling form
